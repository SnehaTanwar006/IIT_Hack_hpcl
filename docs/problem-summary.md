# Problem Summary

HPCL Direct Sales serves industrial customers across sectors such as power, chemicals, fertilizers, shipping, mining, construction, and manufacturing. The hackathon challenge was to build a B2B Lead Intelligence Agent that detects early public signals of procurement, expansion, tendering, and new capacity creation, then converts those signals into actionable sales leads.

The product must recommend HPCL Direct Sales portfolio items such as MS, HSD, LDO, FO, LSHS, SKO, Bitumen, Marine Bunker Fuels, Hexane, Propylene, Jute Batch Oil, Solvent 1425, Mineral Turpentine Oil, and Sulphur.

The expected working prototype includes source ingestion, product inference, lead scoring, lead dossiers, mobile/PWA workflow, WhatsApp or fallback notifications, a feedback loop, model cards, labelled demo data, executive dashboard, and deployment architecture.
